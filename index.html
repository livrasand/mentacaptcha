What is Menta?

Menta is a privacy-first, self-hosted CAPTCHA that runs entirely on your own infrastructure. It uses a hashcash-style proof-of-work challenge to tell humans and bots apart — no third-party scripts, no cookies, no fingerprinting, and no pictures to identify.

Built for developers who want simple bot protection without shipping their users' traffic to a closed service.

Why does it exist?

Menta was born from the need to protect forms and APIs from bots without surrendering visitor data to a third party. Most CAPTCHA services force users to identify traffic lights, train machine-learning models, or hand over fingerprints to a cloud vendor. Menta is a small, open-source, self-hosted alternative: you run it on your own server, your backend verifies the token, and your users' data stays between you and them.

How it works

Menta runs 8 main validation checks across the /init, /challenge and /redeem endpoints:

  1. Rate limit per IP and endpoint. Prevents brute-force requests from a single address.
  2. Bot signal checks. Flags traffic patterns that look automated.
  3. Session validity. Verifies the session exists, has not expired, has not been reused, and matches the original IP.
  4. Session signature. HMAC check proves the session was created by this server.
  5. Challenge signature. HMAC check proves the challenge was created by this server.
  6. Nonce entropy. Ensures the nonce is not a trivial or precomputed value.
  7. Proof-of-work. SHA-256(salt + nonce) must meet the current target difficulty.
  8. Client pattern. Solve time is analyzed against a normal human range to catch scripted shortcuts.

Privacy & compliance

Menta is built with privacy-by-design principles, not as a legal certification. The technical foundation supports strong data protection, but formal compliance depends on your deployment, jurisdiction and legal process.

  • Data minimization. IPs are truncated to /24 (IPv4) or /64 (IPv6) by default. No cookies, no fingerprinting, no third-party telemetry.
  • Retention and cleanup. Configurable data-retention window with automatic cleanup of rate limits, solved challenges, failed attempts and support tickets.
  • Subject rights. Export and delete endpoints for data subject requests.
  • Encryption at rest. Optional SQLCipher encryption when DB_ENCRYPTION_KEY is set.
  • Accessibility. ARIA labels, keyboard support, live regions and focus management as a baseline.
  • Internationalization. Widget text is fully configurable, so integrators can provide their own translations.

Get started

You have three ways to use Menta:

Option 1: Use the hosted widget (SaaS)

Add the script and the <menta-widget> tag to your page. Every new SaaS account gets a 45-day free trial; after that, activate with a simple manual crypto verification.

<script src="https://mentacaptchaeu.eu.pythonanywhere.com/menta-captcha.js"></script>

<menta-widget data-cap-api-endpoint="https://mentacaptchaeu.eu.pythonanywhere.com" data-cap-i18n-initial-state="I'm not a robot"></menta-widget>

Option 2: Self-host

Deploy the backend on any server that supports Python, such as PythonAnywhere or your own VPS. Self-hosted Menta is completely free — no hidden fees, no feature limits, no telemetry.

Option 3: Protect any page with Menta Shield

No captcha widget, no domain registration. If you just want to keep bots away from a page, add one script tag. Menta Shield silently checks every visitor against the bot shield (known AI/SEO crawlers verified by IP ranges or reverse-DNS, robots.txt compliance, generic script user-agents) and decides:

  • Verified bots that disobey your robots.txt → blocked with an access-denied screen.
  • Suspicious clients (curl, python-requests, headless browsers, bot impersonators) → silently solve an invisible proof-of-work challenge before the page is revealed.
  • Everyone else → pass through with no visible challenge at all.
<script src="https://mentacaptchaeu.eu.pythonanywhere.com/menta-shield.js"
        data-menta-api="https://mentacaptchaeu.eu.pythonanywhere.com"></script>

The clearance is cached for 24 hours, so returning visitors are not re-checked. Optional attributes: data-menta-hide-until-pass="true" blurs the page content until verification completes, data-menta-ttl sets the cache duration in seconds, and data-menta-i18n-blocked-title, data-menta-i18n-blocked-detail and data-menta-i18n-checking let you customize the messages. Your backend can also validate the clearance server-to-server via POST /shield/verify.
